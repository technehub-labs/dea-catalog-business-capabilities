# Business Capability

> An enduring ability an enterprise possesses or requires to produce, enable, control, preserve or realize a meaningful business outcome. Coordinated by the ECF, not generated by it.

**Layer:** L3 · **Building Block:** L3-value-delivery · **Entity:** `CAP` · **Model:** [OpenDEAM v0.2.1](https://github.com/technehub-labs/dea-architecture-framework)

[![View in Metamodel Explorer](https://img.shields.io/badge/Metamodel%20Explorer-View%20Entity-2C3E50?style=shield)](https://technehub-labs.github.io/metamodel/?entity=CAP)

## What this is

The canonical catalog of **first-order business capabilities**: abilities sufficiently fundamental and enterprise-general that they can be recognized across materially different enterprises without requiring an industry-specific interpretation.

The catalog answers one question:

> What enduring abilities does an enterprise need to possess or establish to perform as an enterprise?

The [ECF](https://github.com/technehub-labs/dea-metaframework) (Enterprise Composition Framework, 7 Domains × 7 Stages) answers a different question: where a capability primarily operates within the enterprise's domain/lifecycle structure. ECF coordinates are classification context, not capability identity.

This repository also hosts **specialization views** for specific enterprise types (for example a Mobile Communications Service Provider). Specializations refine first-order capabilities; they never enter the first-order set.

## What this catalog is not

| Not this | Because |
|---|---|
| A process taxonomy | "Invoice Customer" is an activity; "Billing Management" is the enduring ability. The metamodel relates Business Capability to Business Process; it does not equate them. |
| An ECF cell filling exercise | The ECF is an organizing framework; capabilities attach to cells where semantics legitimately intersect. No 49 buckets are manufactured from 49 cells. |
| A copy of an external framework | APQC PCF, Business Architecture Guild, and OMG business architecture material are evidence sources, not templates. |
| An organizational chart, system inventory, or outcome list | Each of those is a distinct construct with its own catalog. |

## Key Tenets

1. **Capability precedes coordinate.** Capabilities are derived from enterprise evidence; the ECF coordinates them. The ECF does not manufacture capabilities.
2. **Evidence before canon.** Every capability passes an evidence ladder: Candidate → Observed → Corroborated → Normalized → ECF Mapped → Reviewed → Canonical. "We think every enterprise needs X" never becomes "DEA says every enterprise needs X" without that passage.
3. **First-order means enterprise-general.** Recognizable across commercial, non-profit, government, manufacturing, services, and technology enterprises without industry interpretation. Industry, sector, and organization specializations sit below the first-order set.
4. **Ability, not activity.** A capability is durable: it remains recognizable when processes, organizations, or technology change. Candidates failing the Ability, Outcome, or Implementation Independence tests are rejected.
5. **Possession is a spectrum.** An enterprise may require a capability without performing it (outsourcing). Catalog semantics distinguish required / possessed / sourced / realized.
6. **One primary coordinate, honest secondaries.** The primary ECF coordinate marks earliest initiation (ECF lifecycle rule); secondary coordinates record legitimate participation in other stages.
7. **Depth by recursion, not by hierarchy.** Any ECF cell can decompose into a further 7×7 matrix; the catalog does not need an enormous flat subdivision tree.
8. **Specialization is deferred, not denied.** Telecom Customer Management specializes Customer Management. The first-order catalog holds the parent; specialization views hold the children.

## ECF Relationship

```
Business Capability
        │
        ├── has its own identity
        ├── has a business purpose
        ├── produces/enables an outcome
        ├── has a business object focus
        │
        └── maps to ECF
              │
              ├── primary Domain × Stage   (earliest initiation)
              └── secondary coordinates    (legitimate participation)
```

Workflow direction:

```
Enterprise Evidence → Capability Candidate → Capability Test →
Normalization → Canonical Capability → ECF Mapping (Domain × Stage)
```
