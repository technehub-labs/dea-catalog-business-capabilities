## 2. Method (semantic discipline)

Purpose answers **why the enterprise possesses/requires the capability** — distinct from definition (what it is), outcome (what it contributes to), and why_capability (why it is a capability). For each record, the proposed purpose is **derived from admitted evidence**: the record's definition and its evidence-ladder-admitted outcome (the E-level recorded in `why_capability`) bridge into a why-statement. No statement is inferred from the capability's name, ECF coordinate, presumed universality, or generic enterprise assumptions; no text is mechanically copied between fields (each statement reframes rather than repeats; overlaps in wording reflect shared admitted evidence, and are recorded as such in the register).
