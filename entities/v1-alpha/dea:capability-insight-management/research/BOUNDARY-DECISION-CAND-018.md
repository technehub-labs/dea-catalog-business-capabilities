# Boundary Decision: CAND-018 Analytics and Intelligence

CR-DEA-BC-02 carry-forward: the boundary delineation that gates CAND-018's deferral. Machine-readable twin: `boundary-decision-cand-018.yaml`. Date: 2026-09-01.

## The question

Does Analytics and Intelligence stand as a distinct first-order capability, or is it a specialization of Information Management (CAND-017)? CAND-017's own rationale raised the specialization reading ("analytics flagged as potential specialization: data product vs analytical insight"). The gate close-out deferred CAND-018 on exactly this boundary.

## The delineation

| Test | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Business object | Distinct | Information (stewarded) vs Insight (derived). An insight is a produced thing with its own lifecycle, by parity with Plan (Strategic Planning's object). |
| Ability | Distinct | Stewardship verbs (govern, structure, retain, dispose) vs derivation verbs (model, analyze, predict, interpret). No shared verb set. |
| Outcome | Distinct | Trustworthy, findable information vs decisions informed by derived intelligence. Neither implies the other. |
| Partition | Holds | Strong stewardship without analytics (records-driven institutions) and strong analytics on unstewarded external data are both coherent enterprises. The abilities vary independently. |
| Anti-invention | Passes | Recurs across the corpus (SRC-002, SRC-006). Vendor-heavy support noted; the decision is carried by the object and outcome tests, not by vendor model frequency. |

## Verdict

**Reading A adopted: Analytics and Intelligence is a distinct first-order capability.**

Boundary statement: *Information Management stewards information; Analytics and Intelligence derives insight from information, the enterprise's own or external. The boundary is the business object: stewardship ends where derivation begins.*

The specialization reading is rejected: it would require Insight to be a kind of Information, which the object test contradicts. The analytical context changes the ability exercised, not merely the vocabulary.
